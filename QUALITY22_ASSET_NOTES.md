# Source-derived game assets

The runtime is a rendered/interactive game, not a standalone source asset pack. Do not resell or redistribute the recovered library as a separate asset collection.

37 unavailable original image channels were matched by the recorded asset/channel names to official Poly Haven catalogs (CC0); official download size/MD5 and local SHA256 were checked. Matching a named asset/channel does not prove byte identity to the author’s unavailable files. Original asset IDs, catalogs and per-file URLs are recorded in audit/quality22/recovery.

Other tree/lake assets came embedded in the supplied Blender package, with BlenderKit source credits. Asset-specific license clearance is not represented as fully completed. No raw supplied ZIP/Blend/source collection is included in this release. Existing source credits remain at courses/puttclub/credits.md.

The extracted runtime materials use their original UVs, channels and named fields. Eight selected images were reduced to 1024px for mobile; this is documented in audit/quality22/mobile-textures.json. Grass instances were reduced to original-material/color/normal/AO bakes; near trees retain original geometry while far trees are source-rendered view-dependent LODs. Original material noise/translucency are not pixel-identical to Blender.

RGBELoader.js is Three.js r128’s original MIT-licensed loader, matching three.min.js.
