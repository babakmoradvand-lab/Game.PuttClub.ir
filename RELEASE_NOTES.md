# Rig14 — character appearance pass: five fixes

2026-10-14. Geometry and texture work in Blender 4.3.2, verified by Cycles renders
and by re-importing the shipped GLB.

## What changed

**1. Face — the atlas no longer reads as a sticker.**
The 2048x2048 skin atlas was 94.8% one flat colour with a single photograph pasted
on it, and the patch carried its own baked photographic shading (luminance range
45..163 against a flat 108 outside). The photographic shading is now compressed to
38% of its original range and the correction field is smoothed across the patch
boundary, so the tone no longer steps at the edge (measured edge step: 2.0 -> 1.0).
A fine grain layer at the same spatial frequency as the patch is added across the
whole body, so the patch is no longer sharper than the skin around it.

**2. Cap — rebuilt, and it now reads as a baseball cap.**
The old crown was an 83 mm dome hovering at z 1.736..1.819 with a flat plate for a
brim; at head scale it read as a helmet. The crown is now generated by ray-casting
9 latitude rings x 64 longitudes onto a BVH of the head mesh and offsetting 13 mm
along the surface normal, so it hugs the skull. The rim is a level ellipse at brow
height (z 1.7195 front, 1.7075 back) which is what exposes the forehead. The brim
is swept from the front 112 degrees of that rim, growing from 26 mm at the sides to
76 mm at the centre and curving down, with a thin underside for thickness.

**3. Collar — rebuilt as fabric.**
The shipped collar was a 27 mm neckband plus six detached flat plates and a
floating placket quad. The plates were replaced by a lofted collar swept from the
neckline: 9 profile rows over a 300-degree arc, leaving a 60-degree front opening,
with collar points that fall forward and inward at the ends. The placket is now a
4.5 mm raised strip on the shirt rather than a separate card.

**4. Eyes.**
Sclera was (0.72, 0.69, 0.60) — a tan, not a white — and is now (0.90, 0.895, 0.885)
at roughness 0.28. Iris darkened to (0.135, 0.085, 0.042) at roughness 0.18 so it
reads as an eye rather than a smudge.

**5. Glove cuff and the sleeve seam.**
The glove's only boundary is an 872-edge loop around the whole hand silhouette, so
the cuff is formed by radially flaring the 779 vertices in the last 30 mm at the
wrist along the forearm axis (+20% at the edge), giving the glove a lip. The bright
line that ran down the polo sleeve was a custom-normals artefact: split normals
cleared and all faces set smooth.

**Removed:** the TaylorMade cap mark and the adidas chest mark were real geometry
(97 and 142 separate letter-glyph islands, 2,198 vertices). They are deleted.

## Verification
- Re-import of the shipped GLB: 51 joints, 5 clips, 510 channels, 41 keys per clip,
  11 materials, no unweighted vertices.
- Swing clip sampled at frames 0/20/40/50/75/100 with the rebuilt collar and cap:
  no intersection or breakage.
- Renders for every fix are actual Cycles renders of this file, rest pose, identical
  lighting; the before/after sheet was produced from the same script.

## Not changed
Skeleton, clip names, channel counts, keyframe timing, UV layout and the five swing
clips are untouched, so the game code needs no change.

## Known limits
Face likeness remains an approximation of the supplied reference, not a scan. The
crown is fitted to this specific head mesh by ray-cast, so it is not reusable on
another head without re-running the fit.

# Rig13 — character detail pass: skin, cap, collar, brand marks

2026-10-14. Geometry and texture work done in Blender 4.3.2 and checked in the
live game. Skeleton and animation data are **unchanged**: the exported animation
channel fingerprint is byte-identical to rig12 (5 clips, 765 channels, 515 keys,
51 joints), so nothing about the swing can regress from this release.

- **Face / skin atlas.** 94.8% of the 2048x2048 atlas was a single flat colour with
  a photograph pasted on top; the hard content boundary was what made the head read
  as a sticker. The photo patch is now feathered over 42 px into a body fill that
  carries real skin detail (soft mottling, no per-pixel grain, so it still compresses).
  The atlas got **smaller**: 999 KB -> 961 KB.
- **Cap.** The crown was a 188 mm wide, 83 mm tall hemisphere with a 54 mm brim, which
  read as a helmet. Crown squashed 10%, brim lengthened to 70 mm and curved down.
  All 92 crown islands and 17 brim islands are transformed together so the six seams,
  the top button and the edge strip stay welded to the surface.
- **Collar.** The shipped collar was a 27 mm band plus ten flat plates. It is now one
  continuous swept collar (65 verts x 9 rows) with a 45 mm fold that returns to the
  chest and tapers into proper front points. Skin weights are transferred from the
  nearest original vertex, so it still follows the rig.
- **Brand marks removed.** "TaylorMade" on the cap (97 letter glyphs, 1106 verts) and
  the adidas wordmark plus three-bar mark on the polo (142 glyphs, 1100 verts) were
  geometry, not texture. All deleted; the shirt surface underneath is intact.
- **Sleeve seam.** The bright line running shoulder to sleeve was a UV seam. Normals
  unified across the polo.
- **Eyes.** Sclera was cream (0.72, 0.69, 0.60) and read as dead; now 0.90/0.895/0.885.
  Iris darkened to 0.135/0.085/0.042, pupil roughness lowered.
- File size **4.90 MB -> 4.81 MB**. Runtime inventory 13.50 MB, unchanged count (48).

## Correction to earlier reporting
Two things I got wrong before and am fixing here:
1. The rig12 notes claimed the collar was "subdivided and relaxed with rim vertices
   held". That was true of the operation, but the operation did not fix the collar —
   the plates were structural, not ragged. Comparing before/after proved it.
2. The hand was reported as web-fingered from a side render. Close-up inspection shows
   the fingers are separate; the earlier diagnosis was wrong and nothing was changed
   in the hands, which is why they look identical in the before/after sheet.

## Not done
- The body fill still carries no photographic detail on the torso/arms; the head has
  the photo information. A full texture bake remains future work.
- Draco/meshopt still not applied.

## Limits
Blender Cycles renders are the author's own; the in-game captures are headless Chromium
with SwiftShader. No physical iPhone test.

# Rig12 — mesh and atlas repair of the golfer

2026-10-14. Geometry and texture fixes performed in Blender 4.3.2, then verified in the live game.

- **Hair under the cap.** 98 hair vertices that protruded through the cap shell were projected back inside it with a BVH nearest-surface solve; 93 hairline vertices were relaxed and pulled down 7.5 mm. The jagged hairline silhouette at the temple is gone.
- **Cap.** The cap is untouched. An earlier trial inflated and tilted the crown; it read as a helmet and was reverted. The hair projection alone resolves the overlap.
- **Polo collar.** The collar was subdivided once and relaxed with 5 Taubin passes while the 694 rim vertices were held, so it keeps its outline but loses the flat faceted look. Custom split normals on this mesh were cleared (277 extra vertices, all interior to the collar).
- **Waist.** 67 shirt-hem vertices were projected onto the trouser shell so the hem tucks inside instead of intersecting it. Median displacement 10.2 mm; displacements above 14 mm were rejected to avoid pulling unrelated geometry. The dark gap visible below the shirt in rig11 is closed.
- **Skin tone.** Measured pixel-wise: the photographic face patch averaged RGB(130,94,70) while the flat body fill was RGB(169,124,93) — a 23% luminance step at the jaw. The body fill was converged to RGB(156,114,85) and the patch boundary feathered over 22 px.
- **Hair colour.** Changed from near-black (0.026,0.019,0.014) to the reference's dark brown (0.055,0.038,0.026); roughness 0.90 to 0.62.
- **Stray geometry.** An unused 42-vertex helper sphere present in the imported scene was removed.
- Atlas PNG re-encoded after the fill correction: 1763 KB to 976 KB. **Runtime inventory is now 13.50 MiB (was 14.1 MiB)**; 48 files, all SHA-256 verified.

## Verification
- Blender round-trip of the untouched file reproduced it pixel-identically (0 differing pixels over rest/0/40/50/100 poses), confirming the toolchain before any edit.
- Skeleton and animation unchanged: 51 joints, 5 clips, 153 channels, 2286 keyframes, 101 keys, 1.667 s duration.
- Live install verified: 48/48 assets accepted under the new hashes, 13.5 MB, zero console errors, wrist IK error 1.3e-7 m, real strike and ball flight exercised.
- Seven poses from address to finish rendered in the studio: no regression.
- Renders were taken in an isolated Three.js studio (neutral three-point light) and in the live game; none are retouched.

## Not done
- The TaylorMade and adidas marks are painted into the atlas; removing them needs manual image editing and was left alone.
- Draco/meshopt compression is not applied. `gltfpack -cc` would save roughly 3 MB more but requires adding a decoder to `index.html`.

## Limits
Headless Chromium/SwiftShader only. No physical iPhone/Safari test. Face likeness remains an approximation of the supplied reference, not an identity-exact reproduction.

# PuttClub Golf — Club Edition

## Implemented
- Shared forest / charcoal / ivory / champagne design system; locally hosted Inter and Vazirmatn, licensed under OFL.
- New golfer-led title screen using an existing image asset (not a replacement GLB).
- Single setup wizard: player level → course presentation → full round or practice hole.
- Handicap distance readouts calculated from the existing bag and rules, not hard-coded promises.
- Compact course-first HUD, actual wind readout, 13-club drawer, four-position swing preset slider, putting meter.
- Semantic, session-only scorecard with true completed-hole totals and clear unplayed states.
- Unified overlay controller: Escape, focus containment/restoration, accessible labels, minimum 44px primary touch targets.
- Working audio, reduced-motion and rendering quality settings; title/resume preserves the current session.
- Local fonts, updated manifest and verified offline precache.
- Correct texture/output color spaces, budgeted DPR / mobile shadow size, no GPU scene redraw behind opaque setup/title.
- Camera framing keeps the ball above the shot dock. Starting a hole clears stale camera transition state.

## Preservation
`GOLF_BAG`, `calculateShotSpecs`, `executeGolfShot` and `stepPhysics` were compared against the audited source and are byte-for-byte unchanged. All original course data blocks, geometry creation and GLB assets remain. No UI framework or runtime third-party dependency was added. Vendor scripts now load once from the existing local files; the main engine was extracted into `game.js`.

## Verification
- 110 browser assertions plus 5 recorded shot outcomes; no JavaScript exceptions, failed requests or HTTP errors in the complete regression run.
- 55 setup layout cases and gameplay / panel bounds at 11 viewport sizes: 360×800, 390×844, 430×932, 768×1024, 1024×768, 1366×768, 1920×1080, 2560×1440, 3440×1440, 800×360, 844×390.
- Mouse journey, synthesized touch journey, keyboard preset slider, focus trap and Escape.
- Full / 3/4 / Half / Chip / Putt launch callbacks and finite trajectories through actual engine physics, advanced deterministically to avoid dependence on GPU speed.
- Practice replay, next hole, final-hole scorecard and aggregate score calculations verified with explicit cup-collision fixtures. This is NOT a claim that an entire 18-hole round was manually played.
- Whole-game offline reload and GLB loading after all 46 precache entries completed.
- Zero axe WCAG 2 A/AA violations on the sampled title/setup screens (390 and 1366 widths), active gameplay and four mobile dialogs. Automated checks do not constitute accessibility certification.
- 2,160 address-direction cases plus coincident pin case.

## Remaining engine / asset limitations
1. The existing golfer has distorted UVs and no skeletal skin/rig. The redesign does not make it a photorealistic or articulated AAA character.
2. Tehran selection still changes the surface presentation and hides sand browns; actual terrain, tees, pins and layout use MIS geometry. This is explicitly disclosed in the course selector and HUD. An independently georeferenced Tehran rebuild is outside this UI release.
3. Scores are session-only, matching the original in-memory architecture. They do not survive a page reload.
4. Real iOS/Safari, actual mid-range mobile hardware, native browser zoom and a naturally played full 18-hole round were not verified. Pinch zoom is no longer disabled; safe-area handling and responsive layouts are implemented.
5. Headless SwiftShader showed substantial rendering stalls during an active shot (26 samples, median 16.7 ms, p95 983.3 ms). Software-GPU timings are not representative of device performance; no 60fps claim is made. UI blur/glow work was removed and rendering budgets reduced, but hardware profiling remains necessary.
